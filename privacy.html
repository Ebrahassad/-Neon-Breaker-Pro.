Privacy Policy for Neon Breaker Pro

Last updated: August 2026

Neon Breaker Pro ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Neon Breaker Pro mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

1. Information Collection and Use

Neon Breaker Pro does not directly collect or store personal identifying information such as names, emails, or phone numbers. Game progress, high scores, and coins are saved locally on your device.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Unity Ads)

Our app integrates Unity Ads to serve advertisements. Unity Ads may collect certain non-personal device information to optimize ad delivery, such as:

  • Device IP address and hardware identifiers.
  • Ad interaction data and performance metrics.

For more details on Unity Ads privacy compliance, visit: Unity Privacy Policy.

3. Children's Privacy

Our app is not primarily targeted at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact us via email.
